Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Chu Dai Bi ("the App"), developed by Cuong Tran, collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information.

  • Anonymous session identifier – On first launch, an anonymous session is created automatically via Supabase. This identifier is random, contains no personal data, and is used solely to scope your data.
  • Usage analytics – We collect anonymized usage events (e.g. which chants you play, session duration) via PostHog to understand how the App is used and improve it. No personally identifiable information is included in these events.
  • Device information – Basic device metadata (OS version, device type) may be collected as part of analytics.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Prayer reminders you create (title, time, track selection)
  • Chanting history and streak progress
  • Onboarding completion status

3. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App schedules local prayer reminders on your device. Notification scheduling is handled entirely on-device. No notification content or identifiers are transmitted to external servers.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase – Backend service used for anonymous session management. Privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • PostHog – Analytics platform used to collect anonymized usage data. Privacy policy: posthog.com/privacy

5. Data Retention

Anonymous session data and analytics events are retained for up to 12 months. Local device data remains until you uninstall the App or clear its data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. It contains no registration, social features, or targeted advertising.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: cuong.tran@hiq.fi
